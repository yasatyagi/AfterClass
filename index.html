aCAfterClassdesign v0.3

Phase 01 · Product foundation

The campus, after class and during it.

AfterClass is one place for a college's academic and social life: shared archives, an AI study studio, study groups, clubs, events, a scheduler, and profiles students can make their own. The interface stays calm and structured. The personality shows up in the edges: window chrome, poster type, and profiles students fully own.

Single-college MVP Verified members only Desktop-first · 1440 × 1000
visual_direction.txt

Modern nostalgia

Window title bars, docks, and taskbars used as real structure, not as decoration.

Pastel punk

Poster typography, stickers, and slight tilts, used rarely and on purpose.

Paper first

Warm paper background and ink text. Pastels mark meaning, never fill space.

Quiet core

Dashboards and tools stay calm. Profiles are where expression lives.

Needs & pain points

what we design against
P1

Fragmented resources

Notes and PYQs live in chats and drives. Need: one verified, searchable archive with filters by subject and semester.

P2

Passive studying

Reading PDFs doesn't stick. Need: AI tools grounded in the student's own sources, with clear citations.

P3

Invisible clubs

Clubs have no shared stage. Need: real club pages plus a dedicated placement on the dashboard.

P4

Deadline overload

Classes, exams, and events are tracked separately. Need: one scheduler that answers "what should I focus on today?"

P5

Shallow identity

Social apps flatten people. Need: expressive, customisable profiles that stay safely sandboxed.

P6

Context switching

Chatting means leaving work. Need: a floating messenger window that sits alongside the task.

Phase 01 review

Consistency & usability check

  • Primary nav is capped at 11 items in 3 labelled groups plus Home and Settings, so it never needs to scroll at 1000 px tall.
  • Clubs and Events are separate destinations, linked both ways.
  • Every flow ends with a clear success state and a next action.
  • Messaging has two models: a floating window on desktop and a full-screen page on mobile.

Phase 02 · Design system

Ink on paper, pastel with purpose.

Neutral paper is the base layer. Ink handles structure: borders, text, and primary actions. Each pastel has a single job, so its colour always carries meaning.

Visual guidelines

  • Use at most one pastel accent per component, plus ink.
  • Stickers and tilts belong on promotional content only, never on forms or data.
  • Offset ink shadows mark interactivity (hover) or floating layers. Nothing else gets one.
  • Poster typography is limited to event and club art, and stays uppercase.
  • No gradients on UI surfaces. Patterns (dots, stripes) are for poster art only.
  • Mono text is for data: times, sizes, codes, and file types.
  • Profiles may override any of these rules, but only inside the sandbox.
  • Touch targets: 44 px on mobile and 32 px minimum on desktop.

Phase 02 review

Consistency & usability check

  • All brand pastels pass AA with ink text. Tertiary text stays at 5.6:1 or higher.
  • Every interactive component has its six states defined.
  • Window chrome is a single reusable pattern used by dialogs, widgets, and messenger.
